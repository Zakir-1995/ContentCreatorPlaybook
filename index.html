Chapter 3 in detail

The six-stage system

One repeatable loop instead of starting from scratch with every new piece of content.

01

Ideate

Generate ten angles on a topic, then choose the specific one — not the generic one.

02

Draft

Get a structured first draft from AI. It's raw material, not a finished product.

03

Review & inject voice

The stage most beginners skip — and the one that separates your content from everyone else's.

04

Produce

Turn the reviewed draft into its final format using the toolkit from Chapter 2.

05

Publish & repurpose

One long-form piece becomes a thread, clips, a newsletter section, and a carousel.

06

Review performance

Feed what worked back into your prompt library — the loop that makes the whole system self-improving.
